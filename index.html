One tap says you're okay.

Checo is an iPhone app for checking in with family and friends.

Pick the times you'll check in with each person. When the time comes, one tap tells them you're fine. If a check-in is missed, they get a gentle heads-up so they can reach out.

No maps, no tracking, no ads.
